# Reinforcement Learning Labs

Labs and homework from the *Machine Learning 2 — Reinforcement Learning* course at ENIAD Berkane: tabular Q-learning, SARSA, and PPO applied to classic Gymnasium environments.

Four practical works (`TP1`–`TP4`) plus an introductory homework each implement and evaluate one RL algorithm on a Gymnasium environment — from a first Q-table on `CartPole-v1`, through Q-learning on `FrozenLake-v1` and a head-to-head Q-learning vs. SARSA comparison on a custom traffic-light simulation, to a PPO agent on `Taxi-v3` — with recorded video demonstrations and result plots for each.
